# Umaira Florist

Umaira Florist is an online flower shop for the **City of Manila**. Customers can browse bouquets, design their own, order for delivery or store pickup, pay, and follow their order until it arrives. Shop staff manage orders, payments, products and shop settings from the Admin console.

> **Please note:** Emails from the shop (sign-up codes, order updates, password-reset links) may not reach your inbox at the moment. If your sign-up code doesn't arrive, use **Continue with Google**. You can always see your order status on the website.

---

## For customers

### 1. Sign in or create an account
- Anyone can browse **Collections** and product details without an account.
- You need an account to add items to your cart, build a bouquet, check out and track orders.
- **Create Account:** enter your full name, a Philippine mobile number (e.g. 0917 123 4567), your email and a password of at least 8 characters. Then type the 6-digit code sent to your email (valid for 5 minutes).
- **Continue with Google** signs you in without a code.
- Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** to get a reset link by email.

### 2. Choose your flowers
- **Collections:** ready-made bouquets and flowers. Add what you like to your cart.
- **Build Your Bouquet:** create your own in five steps: choose your flowers, add fillers and greenery (optional), choose your wrapping, choose your ribbon, and add a greeting card with your message (optional).

### 3. Check out
In your cart, select the items you want and go to checkout. Then choose how to receive your order:

| | Delivery | Store pickup |
|---|---|---|
| Where | City of Manila only (Districts I–VI). Choose your district and barangay. | At the shop |
| When | From the next day the shop is open: Morning (9 AM–12 PM), Afternoon (1–4 PM) or Evening (5–7 PM) | Every 30 minutes during opening hours. Same-day pickup needs preparation time (usually 2 hours). |
| Fee | Shown at checkout; free above a minimum order amount | Free |

No deliveries or pickups are scheduled on days the shop is closed. You can add a gift message of up to 250 characters.

### 4. Pay
| Method | What to do |
|---|---|
| **GCash** (pay first) | Scan the shop's QR code and send the **exact amount**. Then, on your order page, upload your receipt screenshot with the GCash reference number. The shop checks the payment and confirms your order. If the proof is rejected, you can submit a new one. |
| **Cash on Pickup** | Pay at the shop counter when you collect your order. |
| **Cash on Delivery** | Pay the rider in cash. |

- If GCash shows **"GCash unavailable"**, please choose a cash option.
- Each GCash reference number can be used only once.
- BDO Bank Transfer is no longer available.

### 5. Track and receive your order
- Follow your order in **My Account → Order History**, on **Track Order**, or through the notifications bell.
- **Delivery:** Pending Confirmation → Confirmed → Preparing → On the Way / Out for Delivery → Delivered (with a delivery photo).
- **Pickup:** Pending Confirmation → Confirmed → Preparing → Ready for Pickup → handed over at the shop.
- **When you receive your order, confirm it on your order page**, or report a problem (you can attach a photo). Your order is completed once you confirm.
- Orders cannot be cancelled from the website. Please contact the shop if you need to cancel.

### Your account
- **Profile Details:** update your name, mobile number and saved delivery address.
- **Order History:** see your orders, their payment status, and upload GCash proof.
- You can delete your account from your account page. Your login, saved address and cart are removed; orders you already placed stay in the shop's records.

## For shop staff (Admin console)

Open the website address followed by **/admin** and sign in with your staff account.

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Sales today, pending and active orders, and low-stock items at a glance |
| **Orders** | **Confirm Order** or **Cancel order**, and move orders through each step. Use **Review Payment** for GCash proofs and **Confirm Cash Received** for cash. Use **Confirm Pickup** when a customer collects an order, **Upload POD & Mark Delivered** with a delivery photo, or **Record Failed Attempt**. |
| **Store Management** | Add and edit products and prices, **Restock**, and archive items you no longer sell |
| **Suppliers** | Keep your supplier list (**Add Supplier**) |
| **Reports** | **Generate Report** to download a PDF business report |
| **Shop Settings** | Store details, delivery and pickup options, opening hours, bouquet builder limits, low-stock level, and **Payment Accounts** (GCash) |

**Setting up GCash (Shop Settings → Payment Accounts):** upload the shop's GCash QR code, enter the account name and GCash number, then click **Save Payment Accounts**. Customers can pay with GCash **only when all three are saved**. The badge shows **GCash active** or **GCash not set up**. Removing the QR code makes GCash unavailable right away.

**Rules the system enforces:**
- A GCash order can be confirmed only after its payment is verified.
- A Cash on Pickup order is handed over only after **Confirm Cash Received**.
- A delivery is marked delivered only with a delivery photo.
- Only the customer can mark an order as received or report a problem.
- Cancelling or rejecting an order returns its items to stock. Archived products disappear from the shop, but old orders keep them.
- Only an **Administrator** or **Owner** can verify GCash payments and change Payment Accounts. **Managers** and **Cashiers** can also confirm cash payments.
